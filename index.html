My story

I'm Brazilian and I've been living in the US for a while now. I got into tech when I was nine, mostly by messing around with computers and wanting to know how they actually worked. I never really got bored of it.

By fourteen I was trying to turn it into a real career. I built small things, broke most of them, and figured out what I needed to fix them. A lot of it was just learning as I went.

My dad has been behind me the whole time. He encouraged it even when it was just some kid's hobby, and he's supported me through all of it since. I don't think I would've started as early as I did without him.
